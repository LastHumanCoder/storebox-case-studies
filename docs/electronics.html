The challenge: competing with Amazon, Flipkart and the big PC shops

A computer parts store with a physical shop in Delhi. In April 2026 it got about 430 Google impressions a day, and most of its clicks came from people already searching its name. Searches like "cheapest gaming pc" or "gaming pc under 30000" were held by the marketplaces and the big PC shops.

What Storebox did

1
Product pages
Specs, prices and product schema on every product, so they show in Google Shopping listings and AI answers.
2
Budget category pages
Gaming PCs, graphics cards and monitors organised by budget, the way Indian shoppers search.
3
Price and buying guides
Graphics card and CPU price guides, budget PC builds and "best for gaming" round-ups.
4
Local store pages
Pages for the physical shop, so local searches lead to the website and the store.
