The challenge: one studio vs the national chains

The client offers one-on-one and small group training, with programs for seniors, women and weight loss. It competes in a single township against Crunch, Planet Fitness, Anytime Fitness, Powerhouse, LA Fitness and Club 55: gyms with national brands, big budgets and years of review volume. The studio's edge is personal, physician-led training. The goal was to show up first when nearby people search.
